Restore a backup

Recover from your encrypted .json file. A safety copy of the old vault is kept.

Restore from Google Drive

On a new computer, connect the same Google account, then restore with your diary passphrase. Your local diary is replaced after a safety copy.

How it works: Backups are encrypted before upload. A sync merges entries when the local and cloud vault share the same encryption key. Restoring a different cloud vault requires its passphrase.
